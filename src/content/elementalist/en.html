The Elementalist (Spirit Master) is the only summoner: a spirit fights next to you, every spirit skill gives you an element, and at four elements {{ s('elemental-fusion') }} is ready. Most of the damage comes from Elemental Fusion and the spirits — above all {{ s('summon-ancient-spirit') }} and {{ s('summon-fire-spirit') }}.

The build in one sentence

Buff first, then summon: a spirit copies your stats at the moment it appears. Keep five stacks of {{ s('cold-shock') }}'s Attack buff, open with the buffs and the Ancient Spirit, and let the macro fire Elemental Fusion whenever it is ready.

  • The snapshot rule. A spirit's damage depends on your stats and buffs when you summon it. A buff cast after the summon does nothing for that spirit — never call the Ancient Spirit without your buffs up.
  • Weave the basic attack. Cold Shock gives +2% Attack per hit, up to five stacks. Losing them costs 10% Attack for you and for every spirit you summon; an extra basic attack is worth more than the next summon.
  • Long fights favour you. The class leans on buffs and damage over time, so it gets stronger the longer a fight lasts — and loses the most if you die with everything on cooldown.

This guide follows Evripides' written Spirit Master guide, global edition for Season 1 (aion2sm.com, written at 700k CP, updated September 29), which DankRNG builds his video on; aLuckyRO's global video adds the day-one picks. The guide is adapted from the Taiwan and Korean version — its author will update it after launch.

{% endcall %} {% call section('intro-pvp', 'Overview', 'pvp') %}

In PvP the Elementalist is a kiting class that wins by outlasting: less mobility than a Ranger and less burst than a Sorcerer, but by far the tankiest ranged class, with fear, a dispel and spirits that keep hitting when you are out of range.

  • Fear is control, not a kill tool. Use it to peel and to force out the enemy's {{ s('defiance', False) }}; the damage comes from Elemental Fusion, curses and spirits.
  • Timing decides fights. Know the other class's skills: a defensive used too early or too late loses the fight.
  • {{ s('summon-earth-spirit') }} taunts — and Defiance cannot break a taunt.

The PvP mode follows the "PvP Starter" build of the same guide (updated September 29).

Two-hold play: hold {{ s('cold-shock') }} on LMB and the macro on RMB, steer, and press the rest by hand. The hotbar line that used to sit on RMB moves to T.

1. Hotbar lines

2. In-game macro

Bind the macro to RMB, 10 ms on each step:

{{ macroseq([(skill_data('enhance-spirits-benediction')[lang].name, 'RMB · 10 ms'), (skill_data('elemental-fusion')[lang].name, '10 ms'), (skill_data('jointstrike-corrode')[lang].name, '10 ms')]) }}

3. The fight

  1. Open with {{ s('flame-blessing') }} → {{ s('enhance-spirits-benediction') }} → {{ s('summon-ancient-spirit') }} — buffs land before the summon.
  2. Spirit line and Corrode, then start the macro and settle into the two-hold.
  3. Keep rotating your spirits; recast the buffs as soon as they are ready instead of saving them for the Ancient Spirit.
  4. If the five Cold Shock stacks are about to drop, an extra basic attack beats the next summon.

The weave depends on ping: the author wants 65% Combat Speed or more and under ~60 ms ping. aLuckyRO's advice for the first week: play without the macro until you know the class. Bind "dismiss all spirits" to a key you can reach.

The Elementalist (Spiritmaster) is the only summoner: a spirit fights next to you, every spirit skill gives you an element, and at four elements {{ s('elemental-fusion') }} is ready. {{ s('dimensional-control') }} fires each time a spirit uses its skill, and curses keep ticking on the target.

Leveling in one sentence

Level with {{ s('summon-water-spirit') }} (damage and mana), hold {{ s('cold-shock') }} for MP, and let the in-game macro cycle the Spirit line and the Fusion line.

{% endcall %} {% call section('fight-lvl', t.lvl_combat, 'lvl') %}
  • Spirit: {{ s('summon-water-spirit') }} until the cap — best single-target damage, and it feeds your mana. The others stay at level 1 while leveling.
  • Packs: {{ s('jointstrike-curse') }} (hits up to 4) → {{ s('combustion') }} → {{ s('elemental-fusion') }} with the AoE pick once it's ready.
  • Mana: the main early problem — target mode, {{ s('cold-shock') }} between skills, and end your spirit rotation on the Water Spirit.
  • Staggered targets: {{ s('rapid-scattershot') }}.
  • Skip {{ s('souls-cry') }} — it's a PvP skill.
