The Sorcerer is a ranged glass cannon that casts both fire and ice. Fire does the damage — {{ s('fire-mark') }} marks the target and {{ s('blaze') }} cashes it in — while ice slows, roots and freezes so nothing reaches you. Half of your damage bonuses depend on mana: above 50% MP you crit more, above 25% you deal 20% more damage.

The build in one sentence

Hold one macro that keeps {{ s('element-enhancement') }} and {{ s('wish-of-concentration') }} running and alternates {{ s('flame-arrow') }} with your fire and ice lines so MP stays above half; cast {{ s('hellfire') }} by hand.

  • Play in target mode. With a locked target the basic attack {{ s('flame-arrow') }} keeps firing by itself after every skill and restores MP. In action mode it stops after each skill and you have to click again.
  • Two stages. In the first days at 45 your skills sit at 8–10 with one specialization each — take the mana and speed picks named in the cards. The card levels and the two highlighted picks are the later target (16), from EUTOPIA's global setup and Korean guides.

The early setup and the macro follow aLuckyRO's Sorcerer beginner guide (video, September 15, Taiwan client) and his global update of October 2; the skill priority and the level-16 picks follow EUTOPIA's global build (level 45, item level 1,260, October 3), who tops the damage charts in his groups. Skill names and data are from the global client.

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for the Sorcerer:

  • 4 stigma slots, capped at 20. Taiwan and Korea play six; the four kept here are the ones both sources level first.
  • Skills at 16, not 20. Arcana and rings are what push skills past 16, and they come slowly.
  • Mana is tight at the start. About 3,000 MP and no cooldown reduction: the early picks are about MP and Skill Speed, not damage.
  • PvP: everyone starts on the same limited gear and no meta has formed yet.

  • Priority: orange Combat Speed and Cooldown Reduction, then Attack and Damage Boost, then Critical Damage Boost; after that the blue nodes of {{ s('hellfire') }}, {{ s('firestorm') }}, {{ s('bittercold-wind') }}, {{ s('blaze') }}, {{ s('wish-of-concentration') }} and the green {{ s('robe-of-earth') }}.

{% endcall %} {% call section('macro', 'Macro & rotation', 'pve') %}

AION 2 has two separate tools:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar buttons in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

1. Hotbar lines

{% call hotline('4', 'Buff line', ['element-enhancement', 'bittercold-wind', 'delayed-explosion']) %}Element Enhancement goes off whenever it is ready; then the root field and the +15% damage debuff.{% endcall %} {% call hotline('5', 'Concentration line', ['wish-of-concentration', 'cold-storm', 'fire-wall']) %}The second buff first, then both fields under the target.{% endcall %} {% call hotline('1', 'Fire line', ['blaze', 'firestorm']) %}Blaze whenever the target carries a Fire Mark, Firestorm on its 5-second cooldown.{% endcall %} {% call hotline('T', 'Ice line', ['winters-shackles', 'ice-chain']) %}Winter's Shackles for the damage buff; Ice Chain fills the rest if you kept it — without it the line is Winter's Shackles alone.{% endcall %}

{{ s('flame-arrow') }} sits alone on R, {{ s('hellfire') }} on 2, {{ s('flame-scattershot') }} on 3.

2. In-game macro

Bind a macro key in Settings → Key settings → Gameplay → Macro. Every step with the same delay:

{{ macroseq([('Buff line', '4'), ('Concentration line', '5'), (skill_data('flame-arrow')[lang].name, 'R'), ('Fire line', '1'), (skill_data('flame-arrow')[lang].name, 'R'), ('Ice line', 'T'), (skill_data('flame-scattershot')[lang].name, '3')]) }}
  • Delay: 10 ms with a ping under 50; 40–50 ms with a ping of 80–100 and more.
  • How to check it: watch your character while the macro runs. Afterimages ("shadows") behind it mean animations are being cancelled and the delay is fine.
  • Why two Flame Arrows: they refill mana between the expensive skills, so MP stays above 50% and both mana passives stay on.

The steps come from aLuckyRO's macro window (September 15, Taiwan client); Frost and Frost Burst were taken out after EUTOPIA's global setup. Flame Scattershot as the last step follows what aLuckyRO says (it fires by itself on a staggered boss).

3. What you actually press

  • HoldThe macro key — nothing else.
  • By hand{{ s('hellfire') }}: stop, charge it fully, go back to the macro.
  • Low manaIf MP still drops, let go of the macro, fire a few {{ s('flame-arrow') }}s and continue.

4. Later: burst by hand

Once your gear carries cooldown reduction, Korean players take the buffs out of the macro and open a burst window by hand:

  1. {{ s('delayed-explosion') }} just before the tank pulls.
  2. {{ s('element-enhancement') }} and {{ s('wish-of-concentration') }}.
  3. {{ s('fire-wall') }} + {{ s('cold-storm') }} under the boss.
  4. {{ s('bittercold-wind') }}, then {{ s('winters-shackles') }} for the +20% damage boost.
  5. {{ s('hellfire') }} charged to 3, then the macro.
