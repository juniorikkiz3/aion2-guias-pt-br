In AION 2 dungeons the Cleric is a healer that has to deal damage: rewards depend on your contribution, and a Cleric who only heals can walk out with nothing. Your party has plenty of dodges and invulnerabilities of its own — top people up after hits, and put everything else into damage.

The build in one sentence

{{ s('earth-punishment') }} makes {{ s('condemnation') }} always crit, and a crit resets Condemnation — so it fires with no cooldown, while {{ s('divine-aura') }}, {{ s('bolt') }} and {{ s('noble-aura') }} add damage on top.

This mode follows Kaeria's Cleric PvE video guide (Russian, recorded on the Taiwan client on September 28; she says it applies to global too), checked against the video frames on September 30, and against Lucia's guide (English, global, October 5 — a World's First Rank 1 Cleric at 2,160 item level). Skill picks are reversible for free — only arcana cost resources.

{% endcall %} {% call section('intro-pvp', 'Overview', 'pvp') %}

In PvP the Cleric keeps the PvE core — {{ s('condemnation') }} reset by crits, {{ s('judgment-thunder') }}, the heals — and changes what the stigmas and a few picks are for: crowd control, cutting the enemy's healing, and keeping the party alive through a burst.

The build in one sentence

Buff with {{ s('prayer-of-amplification') }} and {{ s('light-of-protection') }}, put {{ s('voice-of-doom') }} on the healer or the tank you are killing, and save {{ s('yustiels-power') }} for the moment the enemy bursts.

This mode follows the PvP part of Lucia's Cleric guide (English, global, October 5 — a World's First Rank 1 Cleric): stigmas for duels and group fights and the picks she changes for PvP. She promised a separate video on PvP combos; until then the rotation is the PvE one.

{% endcall %} {% call section('global', 'Global Season 1: what changes', 'pve pvp') %}

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for the Cleric:

  • 4 stigma slots, capped at 20. Kaeria's Taiwan bar has more; the four in this guide are her picks for global. The level-25 tiers (such as Double Chance on Light of Protection) don't exist here.
  • You are paid for damage. Dungeon rewards depend on your contribution, so a Cleric who only heals can leave with nothing — this whole build is about dealing damage while healing on the side.
  • Bigger groups (5-player expeditions, 10-player sanctuaries): with a Chanter in the party your mantra doesn't stack with theirs — swap Light of Protection out.
  • Lower ping than on Taiwan: animations cancel better, so the macro can weave more basic attacks.

Active skills and passives are identical in the global and KR/TW data; stigmas lose their level-25 tier. The source video was recorded on the Taiwan client with English skill names.

{% endcall %} {% call section('passives', 'Passives', 'pve') %}
  • {{ s('empyrean-lords-grace') }} — the most important one: Critical Damage, Double Chance and extra damage on every hit. As high as you can.
  • {{ s('earths-grace') }} — used to buff the party, now only you; still worth leveling.
  • {{ s('healing-enhancement') }} — heal boost as a share of your Attack, so a Cleric builds Attack rather than Defense.
  • {{ s('radiant-benediction') }} — heals nearby allies on attacks; take it if you go all-in on damage.
  • {{ s('warm-benediction') }} and {{ s('immortal-veil') }} are fine to have; {{ s('empyrean-lords-benediction') }} is for PvP.

Lucia levels these three first, before any other passive: {{ s('empyrean-lords-grace') }}, {{ s('healing-enhancement') }} and {{ s('earths-grace') }}. {{ s('immortal-veil') }} helps early and falls off once your armor scales.

First days (aLuckyRO): level {{ s('radiant-benediction') }} — it heals the whole party every few seconds while they hit — and {{ s('healing-enhancement') }} first. The crit passives pay off only once your gear gives Critical Hit; until then you barely crit.

{% endcall %} {% call section('gear', 'Gear & stats', 'pve') %}
  • Gear lines: Might (attack and heal), Combat Speed, Damage Boost; Weapon Damage Boost and Precision are strong too. Skip status resists, evasion and defensive lines in PvE.
  • Weapon and guard (Lucia): Weapon Damage Boost, Might, Combat Speed, Precision. Gloves: Combat Speed; boots: Movement Speed. Accessories: Combat Speed, Might and Precision, plus one line for Earth's Grace or Empyrean Lord's Grace.
  • Rings: craft them as soon as you can — crafted rings have one more line for active skills. Fill every line with active skills: Condemnation, Radiant Recovery, Judgment Thunder, and Healing Light or Divine Aura.
  • Stats: Destruction, Wisdom (Double Chance), Illusion (cooldowns), Time (Combat Speed), Freedom (accuracy), plus Death (Critical Hit) — Kaeria's own pick, a Cleric is always short on crit; Might, Precision, Constitution. Lucia's four: Time, Illusion, Space, Destruction.
  • For the Ludra raid: at least 1,000 Accuracy and 1,000 Critical Hit.
  • Wings: Talisra Wings (Attack 60, Defense 400, Accuracy 35, −4% cooldowns) from the season shop for about 250,000 season points — save the points for them.

{% endcall %} {% call section('daevanion', 'Daevanion boards', 'pve pvp') %}

Daevanion takes your skills from 10 to 14 and adds stats. Each blue node gives +1 level to its skill.

  • Priority: orange Cooldown and Combat Speed nodes first, then Attack and Critical, then the blue skill nodes, then the defensive orange nodes, passives last.
  • Critical Damage Tolerance and Multi-Hit Resist do nothing in PvE — bosses don't crit or multi-hit.
  • Resetting a board is cheap, and you will rebuild it many times.
  • Lucia's order: on Nezekan and Zikel take all four corners and your skills; on Vaizel and Triniel skip the corners and go straight for the active skills you use.
  • Whelps' order: first the blue nodes that take your active skills to 12 — the second specialization slot is a big power spike; then the orange attack nodes (Combat Speed, Cooldown Reduction, Damage Boost, Critical Damage Boost, Multi-Hit Chance), the Empyrean Lord's Grace and Earth's Grace nodes, Attack and Critical, and only then the defensive ones (Damage Tolerance, Critical Damage Tolerance, HP, Defense). Avoid the MP +50 nodes where you can.
  • Azphel (level 45) is all PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — take it for PvP.
  • No Ariel on global: there are five boards, and the level-45 one, Azphel, gives only PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — in PvE finish the first four.

The Cleric is the healer, but while leveling it plays as a ranged damage dealer: {{ s('chain-of-torment') }} marks the target, {{ s('condemnation') }} punishes it, and the passive {{ s('radiant-benediction') }} heals you and the party on every hit. Heal only when you need to.

Leveling in one sentence

Hold {{ s('earths-retribution') }} for mana, spam the Chain line with the in-game macro, and put your skill points into attacks rather than heals.

  • Elites: {{ s('debilitating-mark') }} (lower Defense) → {{ s('chain-of-torment') }} → {{ s('bolt') }} charged → {{ s('condemnation') }}.

  • Skill points: the attack skills first, heals later; spare points go into {{ s('empyrean-lords-grace') }} and {{ s('earths-grace') }} (Whelps).
