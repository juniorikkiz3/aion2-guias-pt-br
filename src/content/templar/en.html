Templar is AION 2's main tank, and it still deals real damage. Its whole build rests on a "holy trinity" of skills taken to 20 as fast as possible — {{ s('judgment') }}, {{ s('punishment') }} and {{ s('pummel') }} — that you use as often as you can. Survivability is not what limits a Templar; damage is.

The build in one sentence

Shield skills trigger {{ s('judgment') }}: hold the macro — it opens with {{ s('doom-shield') }}, keeps Judgment going and fills the gaps with Pummel and its procs — charge {{ s('punishment') }} on cooldown, and block the big hits to keep {{ s('fury') }} on the party.

This guide follows SolAshur's global-launch Templar build (solashur.com, September 27) and his video walkthrough of it (September 29), re-checked against the video frames on September 30.

{% endcall %} {% call section('intro-pvp', 'Overview', 'pvp') %}

In PvP the Templar keeps the same holy trinity and the same Judgment loop; what changes is the stigmas. {{ s('executing-blade') }} gives you an edge in every duel, and {{ s('annihilate') }} at 20 turns into an AoE knockdown.

Melee gets kited — a strong weapon matters less when you can't reach the target, so play around your gap-closers and Doom Shield.

Dedicated PvP gear sets from KR/TW are not confirmed for global, so the PvX gear from the PvE tab is the plan. If PvP gear does appear: level your PvP stigmas first, and only then spend on a PvP weapon or armor.

{% endcall %} {% call section('global', 'Global Season 1: what changes', 'pve pvp') %}

Global servers open on October 5, 2026 (Founder's early access from September 30). What that means for Templar:

  • 4 stigmas at level 45. SolAshur's Taiwan loadouts had six; for global he drops two per profile (listed in Stigmas).
  • Create three skill presets before launch — PvX, PvP and PvE — so switching content is one click.
  • Collect Skill Point Shards early: they gate how fast the holy trinity reaches 20.
  • Join a legion — solo Abyss farming is ineffective.
  • Check stigma names at launch: some may be renamed in the global client.

{% endcall %} {% call section('gear', 'Gear path', 'pve') %}

If you start on day one, this is the route:

  1. Holy Spirit set ({{ it('{i:holyspirit_torso}') }} for Elyos, in Verteron and Eltnen; Asmodians get the same gear as the Spectral set — {{ it('{i:spectral_torso}') }} — in Altgard and Morheim. Elyos and Asmodian gear is identical, only the names differ; hover an item to compare the stats) — farm it in the lower Abyss (AP, pets and gold drops per kill, plus boss spawns) or anywhere in the open world. Aim for about 1,600 combat power.
  2. Skip the first dungeon tier: Urugugu Canyon for the Aulamus Longsword (item level 76, attack 308–385, binds on pickup) and Vakron Sky Island for the Vakron Guard (item level 76) and the Vakron armor. Keep upgrading the Vakron pieces until Fire Temple is doable.
  3. Use Transcendence to get ready for Fire Temple — the Enraged Kromede Sword (item level 84, attack 340–425, extended range) — and Ferocious Horn (Nuakum armor). Nuakum is the safer farm: nearly every piece is an upgrade, while the weapon chest can drop other classes' weapons.
  4. End goal — PvE: White Dragon Lord sword, then the guard, then the accessories, with Nuakum armor (full White Dragon armor only adds PvE tolerance and is whale territory). PvX, the setup for most players: Enraged Kromede sword, White Dragon guard, Nuakum armor, White Dragon accessories.

Wings: Dark Veil early (attack bonus, damage boost), Talisra late — Attack 60, Defense 400, Accuracy 35 and 4% cooldown reduction. Stack cooldown reduction as early as you can: shorter shield-skill cooldowns mean more Judgment procs. Collect every other wing too — their "owned" effects and flight power help with Abyss farming.

Two stat builds, chosen by what the stats synergize with:

  • A · Block + Evasion + Dexterity — early and mid game, against opponents without much Accuracy. Dexterity feeds both Block and Evasion. SolAshur's pick for launch.
  • B · Block + Endurance + Ironclad Defense — late game: at 100% Endurance a hit deals 50% less damage.

Daevanion takes your skills from 10 to 14 and adds stats. Each blue node gives +1 level to its skill.

  • Priority: blue nodes for {{ s('judgment') }}, {{ s('punishment') }}, {{ s('pummel') }}, then {{ s('shield-smite') }} and {{ s('annihilate') }}; orange nodes next.
  • Skip: MP nodes (put the points into Attack), {{ s('flash-rampage') }} nodes, and the green Warding Shield / Guardian Shield nodes unless you have spare points.
  • No Ariel on global: there are five boards, and the level-45 one, Azphel, gives only PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — in PvE finish the first four.
  • Azphel (level 45) is all PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — take it.

{% endcall %} {% call section('macro', 'Macro & rotation', 'pve') %}

AION 2 has two separate tools:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar lines in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

Leonis' global setup (video of Oct 5, frame 0:57): two hotbar lines that the in-game macro presses, the rest by hand. Doom Shield now opens the macro; he says the updated setup gives about 20% more damage.
