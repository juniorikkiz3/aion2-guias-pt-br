Overview

Templar is AION 2's main tank, and it still deals real damage. Its whole build rests on a "holy trinity" of skills taken to 20 as fast as possible — Judgment, Punishment and Pummel — that you use as often as you can. Survivability is not what limits a Templar; damage is.

The build in one sentence

Shield skills trigger Judgment: hold the macro — it opens with Doom Shield, keeps Judgment going and fills the gaps with Pummel and its procs — charge Punishment on cooldown, and block the big hits to keep Fury on the party.

This guide follows SolAshur's global-launch Templar build (solashur.com, September 27) and his video walkthrough of it (September 29), re-checked against the video frames on September 30.

Patch Season 1. The build is checked against guides from the global servers: 4 stigmas, cap 20.

Overview

In PvP the Templar keeps the same holy trinity and the same Judgment loop; what changes is the stigmas. Executing Blade gives you an edge in every duel, and Annihilate at 20 turns into an AoE knockdown.

Melee gets kited — a strong weapon matters less when you can't reach the target, so play around your gap-closers and Doom Shield.

Dedicated PvP gear sets from KR/TW are not confirmed for global, so the PvX gear from the PvE tab is the plan. If PvP gear does appear: level your PvP stigmas first, and only then spend on a PvP weapon or armor.

Gear path

If you start on day one, this is the route:

  1. Holy Spirit set (Holy Spirit Breastplate for Elyos, in Verteron and Eltnen; Asmodians get the same gear as the Spectral set — Spectral Breastplate — in Altgard and Morheim. Elyos and Asmodian gear is identical, only the names differ; hover an item to compare the stats) — farm it in the lower Abyss (AP, pets and gold drops per kill, plus boss spawns) or anywhere in the open world. Aim for about 1,600 combat power.
  2. Skip the first dungeon tier: Urugugu Canyon for the Aulamus Longsword (item level 76, attack 308–385, binds on pickup) and Vakron Sky Island for the Vakron Guard (item level 76) and the Vakron armor. Keep upgrading the Vakron pieces until Fire Temple is doable.
  3. Use Transcendence to get ready for Fire Temple — the Enraged Kromede Sword (item level 84, attack 340–425, extended range) — and Ferocious Horn (Nuakum armor). Nuakum is the safer farm: nearly every piece is an upgrade, while the weapon chest can drop other classes' weapons.
  4. End goal — PvE: White Dragon Lord sword, then the guard, then the accessories, with Nuakum armor (full White Dragon armor only adds PvE tolerance and is whale territory). PvX, the setup for most players: Enraged Kromede sword, White Dragon guard, Nuakum armor, White Dragon accessories.

Wings: Dark Veil early (attack bonus, damage boost), Talisra late — Attack 60, Defense 400, Accuracy 35 and 4% cooldown reduction. Stack cooldown reduction as early as you can: shorter shield-skill cooldowns mean more Judgment procs. Collect every other wing too — their "owned" effects and flight power help with Abyss farming.

Titles — which one to equip in each slot: After level 45 → Titles. Pets and Genus Insight — what to level and what to roll at 10: Pets → Genus Insight.

Daevanion boards

Daevanion takes your skills from 10 to 14 and adds stats. Each blue node gives +1 level to its skill.

  • Priority: blue nodes for Judgment, Punishment, Pummel, then Shield Smite and Annihilate; orange nodes next.
  • Skip: MP nodes (put the points into Attack), Flash Rampage nodes, and the green Warding Shield / Guardian Shield nodes unless you have spare points.
  • Ariel (new, October 21): a sixth board, for PvE. Its points don't come from levels: Ariel Fragments drop in the Hard mode of Krao Cave and Draupnir (item level 2600) and, since October 28, of Urugugu Canyon and Vakron Sky Island (2700), and 100 fragments become one Daevanion Crystal: Ariel in Substance Morph (Special → Daevanion) — up to 30 crystals from each dungeon, so 120 points per character. Its route is on the Ariel tab of the board below. Azphel stays PvP-only.
  • Azphel (level 45) is all PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — take it.

Macro & rotation

?The macro key is not bound by default: bind it in Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro. Use the mouse button the guide names — usually RMB, held together with the basic attack on LMB.

AION 2 has two separate tools:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar lines in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

Leonis' global setup (video of Oct 5, frame 0:57): two hotbar lines that the in-game macro presses, the rest by hand. Doom Shield now opens the macro; he says the updated setup gives about 20% more damage.

Leonis' bar is a damage setup: Noble Armor sits under the Banner on E and Taunt is not on it. With the stigmas from the overview (Taunt in a group), put Taunt on a free key — X or R — and press it by hand; the macro never needs it.

Recap

  • Skills: Judgment, Punishment, Pummel 20 first; Annihilate and Shield Smite 16; the rest 12.
  • Stigmas: Empyrean Lord's Punishment, Battlefield Banner, Doom Shield, Taunt — 1 point in each, then the breakpoints.
  • Stats: Block + Evasion + Dexterity early, Block + Endurance late; don't skip pets.
  • Gear: Holy Spirit / Spectral → Urugugu / Vakron → Fire Temple and Ferocious Horn → White Dragon weapon, guard and accessories.

Recap

  • Skills: the same trinity at 20, plus Annihilate 20 for the AoE knockdown.
  • Stigmas: Executing Blade, Empyrean Lord's Punishment, Doom Shield, Nezekan's Shield (Second Skin vs magic, Comrade in Arms for groups).
  • Pantheon: Time (attack speed) instead of Illusion.

How to fight while leveling

  • Packs: Poach one in (a kill resets it) → Warding Strike → hold Vicious Strike + the Pummel macro.
  • Elites: charged Punishment → Shield Smite (stun) → Judgment → Annihilate.
  • Mana: Vicious Strike restores MP and Pummel spends it — alternate them and you never run dry.
  • Low HP: Warding Strike heals and cuts damage; every block heals through Warding Shield.
