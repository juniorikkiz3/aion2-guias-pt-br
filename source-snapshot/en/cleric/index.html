Overview

In AION 2 dungeons the Cleric is a healer that has to deal damage: rewards depend on your contribution, and a Cleric who only heals can walk out with nothing. Your party has plenty of dodges and invulnerabilities of its own — top people up after hits, and put everything else into damage.

  • You are paid for damage. Dungeon rewards depend on your contribution, so a Cleric who only heals can leave with nothing — this whole build is about dealing damage while healing on the side.

The build in one sentence

Earth Punishment makes Condemnation always crit, and a crit resets Condemnation — so it fires with no cooldown, while Divine Aura, Bolt and Noble Aura add damage on top.

This mode follows Kaeria's Cleric PvE video guide (Russian, recorded on the Taiwan client on September 28; she says it applies to global too), checked against the video frames on September 30, and against Lucia's guide (English, global, October 5 — a World's First Rank 1 Cleric at 2,160 item level). The endgame setup — the Condemnation and Bolt picks, skill levels, the macro with two presets (with and without a Chanter), stigma order and arcana — follows Whelps' update of October 20 (global). Skill picks are reversible for free — only arcana cost resources.

Patch Season 1. The build is checked against guides from the global servers: 4 stigmas, cap 20.

Overview

In PvP the Cleric keeps the PvE core — Condemnation reset by crits, Judgment Thunder, the heals — and changes what the stigmas and a few picks are for: crowd control, cutting the enemy's healing, and keeping the party alive through a burst.

The build in one sentence

Buff with Prayer of Amplification and Light of Protection, put Voice of Doom on the healer or the tank you are killing, and save Yustiel's Power for the moment the enemy bursts.

This mode follows the PvP part of Lucia's Cleric guide (English, global, October 5 — a World's First Rank 1 Cleric): stigmas for duels and group fights and the picks she changes for PvP. She promised a separate video on PvP combos; until then the rotation is the PvE one.

Passives

  • Empyrean Lord's Grace — the most important one: Critical Damage, Double Chance and extra damage on every hit. As high as you can.
  • Earth's Grace — used to buff the party, now only you; still worth leveling.
  • Healing Enhancement — heal boost as a share of your Attack, so a Cleric builds Attack rather than Defense.
  • Radiant Benediction — heals nearby allies on attacks; take it if you go all-in on damage.
  • Warm Benediction and Immortal Veil are fine to have; Empyrean Lords' Benediction is for PvP.

Lucia levels these three first, before any other passive: Empyrean Lord's Grace, Healing Enhancement and Earth's Grace. Immortal Veil helps early and falls off once your armor scales.

First days (aLuckyRO): level Radiant Benediction — it heals the whole party every few seconds while they hit — and Healing Enhancement first. The crit passives pay off only once your gear gives Critical Hit; until then you barely crit.

Gear & stats

  • Gear lines: Might (attack and heal), Combat Speed, Damage Boost; Weapon Damage Boost and Precision are strong too. Skip status resists, evasion and defensive lines in PvE.
  • Weapon and guard (Lucia): Weapon Damage Boost, Might, Combat Speed, Precision. Gloves: Combat Speed; boots: Movement Speed. Accessories: Combat Speed, Might and Precision, plus one line for Earth's Grace or Empyrean Lord's Grace.
  • Rings: craft them as soon as you can — crafted rings have one more line for active skills. Fill every line with active skills: Condemnation, Radiant Recovery, Judgment Thunder, and Healing Light or Divine Aura.
  • Stats: Destruction, Wisdom (Double Chance), Illusion (cooldowns), Time (Combat Speed), Freedom (accuracy), plus Death (Critical Hit) — Kaeria's own pick, a Cleric is always short on crit; Might, Precision, Constitution. Lucia's four: Time, Illusion, Space, Destruction.
  • For the Ludra raid: at least 1,000 Accuracy and 1,000 Critical Hit.
  • Wings: start with the Forest Spirit wings — the cheapest and the best for boss damage (Whelps); anything but the Dark Veil boss wings works. Best in slot: Talisra Wings (Attack 60, Defense 400, Accuracy 35, −4% cooldowns) from the season shop for about 250,000 season points — save the points for them. Collect every wing in the end for their owned effects.
  • Cooldown reduction: the more the better — at about 33% Condemnation would never stop proccing, but in Season 1 about 10–11% in total is what you can reach (Whelps).
  • Titles (Whelps): attack — Guardian of Baran or Artguard for PvE Damage Boost until Unbound by Genus (every Genus wheel at 10); defense — Saint for Damage Tolerance and Defense; other — Through Heaven Back for cooldown reduction until Empty Closet (Combat Speed and cooldown reduction, for 700 skins — extract blue and lower gear and combine, the closet is shared with your alts).

Arcana

Whelps' rule for Season 1 (October 20): realistically one or two skills reach 20 — a third or fourth needs god rolls and isn't worth it, and the cards will most likely be redone in Season 2. So aim for Condemnation 20 only — it does about half of your damage — and take a second skill to 20 only when the cards give you an angle. Once Condemnation is 20, go back to Conquest and farm kina and uniques for your soulbind rolls.

Daevanion boards

Daevanion takes your skills from 10 to 14 and adds stats. Each blue node gives +1 level to its skill.

  • Priority: orange Cooldown and Combat Speed nodes first, then Attack and Critical, then the blue skill nodes, then the defensive orange nodes, passives last.
  • Critical Damage Tolerance and Multi-Hit Resist do nothing in PvE — bosses don't crit or multi-hit.
  • Resetting a board is cheap, and you will rebuild it many times.
  • Lucia's order: on Nezekan and Zikel take all four corners and your skills; on Vaizel and Triniel skip the corners and go straight for the active skills you use.
  • Whelps' order: first the blue nodes that take your active skills to 12 — the second specialization slot is a big power spike; then the orange attack nodes (Combat Speed, Cooldown Reduction, Damage Boost, Critical Damage Boost, Multi-Hit Chance), the Empyrean Lord's Grace and Earth's Grace nodes, Attack and Critical, and only then the defensive ones (Damage Tolerance, Critical Damage Tolerance, HP, Defense). Avoid the MP +50 nodes where you can.
  • Azphel (level 45) is all PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — take it for PvP.
  • Ariel (new, October 21): a sixth board, for PvE. Its points don't come from levels: Ariel Fragments drop in the Hard mode of Krao Cave and Draupnir (item level 2600), and 100 fragments become one Daevanion Crystal: Ariel in Substance Morph (Special → Daevanion) — up to 30 crystals from each dungeon, so 60 points per character. Its route is on the Ariel tab of the board below. Azphel stays PvP-only.

?The macro key is not bound by default: bind it in Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro. Use the mouse button the guide names — usually RMB, held together with the basic attack on LMB.

Whelps' endgame setup (video of Oct 20): two skill presets — without a Chanter and with a Chanter in the party; each preset keeps its own skill levels, stigmas and bar, so switch the preset, not the skills. The in-game macro has two steps and fires two hotbar lines; in AION 2 the bottom slot of a line has the highest priority, and the cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.

  • Judgment Thunder leaves the macro once Earth Punishment is 20: it takes the animation of Condemnation, your main skill (half of your damage). Right after the change it may feel the same or even weaker — that is because Condemnation and Bolt are not 16 yet; with them at 16 and more Combat Speed and cooldown reduction, the bar without Thunder does clearly more. Without it you also run short of mana less, and more basic attacks proc more Discharge — and every Discharge cuts Bolt's cooldown.

Overview

The Cleric is the healer, but while leveling it plays as a ranged damage dealer: Chain of Torment marks the target, Condemnation punishes it, and the passive Radiant Benediction heals you and the party on every hit. Heal only when you need to.

Leveling in one sentence

Hold Earth's Retribution for mana, spam the Chain line with the in-game macro, and put your skill points into attacks rather than heals.

Patch Season 1. The build is checked against guides from the global servers: 4 stigmas, cap 20.

How to fight while leveling

  • Packs: Chain of Torment (hits up to 4) → Condemnation → Divine Aura with the AoE pick → Judgment Thunder.
  • Elites: Debilitating Mark (lower Defense) → Chain of Torment → Bolt charged → Condemnation.
  • Mana: a locked target, and weave Earth's Retribution — it restores MP on every cast.
  • Low HP: Healing Light, and Radiant Benediction keeps ticking as long as you hit.
  • Staggered bosses: Lightning Strike Scattershot only works while the target is staggered.
  • Skill points: the attack skills first, heals later; spare points go into Empyrean Lord's Grace and Earth's Grace (Whelps).
