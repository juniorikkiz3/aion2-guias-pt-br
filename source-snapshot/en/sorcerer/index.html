Overview

The Sorcerer is a ranged glass cannon that casts both fire and ice. Fire does the damage — Fire Mark marks the target and Blaze cashes it in — while ice slows, roots and freezes so nothing reaches you. Half of your damage bonuses depend on mana: above 50% MP you crit more, above 25% you deal 20% more damage.

The build in one sentence

Open with Wish of Concentration, Element Enhancement, Delayed Explosion and Winter's Shackles, then charge Hellfire fully by hand inside the 5 seconds of Winter's Shackles; the rest runs on stacked hotbar lines or a three-button macro. Once Wish of Concentration reaches 16, it moves to the end.

  • Lock your target. Press Tab or click the enemy — two arrows on its HP bar mean a hard lock. Then the basic attack Flame Arrow keeps firing by itself after every skill and restores MP; if you only aim at the enemy, it stops after each skill and you have to click again. This is about the lock, not the control mode: AION 2 mode works fine (see Settings).
  • Two stages. In the first days at 45 your skills sit at 8–10 with one specialization each — take the mana and speed picks named in the cards. The card levels and the highlighted picks are the later target (16, from the global builds and Korean guides); what to take at 12–15 is in each card's text (EUTOPIA).

The skill priority, the rotation, the macro, the stigma and passive order follow EUTOPIA's build update of October 11 (global, built on global, Korean and Taiwan boss logs and his own A/B tests at gear score 1,800–1,900); the specializations up to 15 and the stacked hotbar lines with their damage tests come from his global build (October 3) and update (October 7) — his skills sit at 14–15 there. The level-16 picks follow the global builds on questlog.gg and the Korean guides. The first-days picks follow aLuckyRO's beginner guide (September 15, Taiwan client) and his global update of October 2. Skill names and data are from the global client.

Passives

PassiveWhy
Robe of FlameAccuracy, PvE Damage Boost and Double Chance — the first passive to max.
Fire MarkApplies Fire Mark, which Blaze needs, and burns the target. Max it.
Grace of Enhancement+20% PvE Damage Boost while MP is 25% or more, and extra hits — EUTOPIA's third priority.
Robe of EarthMax MP and Critical Hit while MP is 50% or more — you mostly are. Max it.
Vitality EvaporationExtra damage while the target is above 70% HP — strong early, when fights are short.
Robe of ColdDefense, Critical Hit Resist and a slow on attackers — EUTOPIA keeps it high for the hard bosses.
Cold SnapExtra damage on slowed targets (Winter's Shackles slows them). Less than it looks — EUTOPIA leaves it low.

Robe of Earth's Critical Hit needs 50% MP or more, Grace of Enhancement's damage boost 25% or more — keep MP above half. Krix Domi (October 14) ranks them differently: Robe of Flame as high as you can, then Robe of Earth for its crit, then Robe of Cold — the rest he calls minor.

Daevanion boards

Daevanion takes your skills from 10 to 14 and adds stats. Each blue node gives +1 level to its skill.

  • Priority: orange Combat Speed and Cooldown Reduction, then Attack and Damage Boost, then Critical Damage Boost; after that the blue nodes in the order you level the skills — Wish of Concentration, Firestorm, Flame Arrow, Blaze, Bittercold Wind, Winter's Shackles, then Hellfire (at least to 12) — plus Flame Scattershot and the green Fire Mark, Grace of Enhancement and Robe of Earth (EUTOPIA, October 11).
  • Skip Multi-Hit and the defensive orange nodes — a Sorcerer wants flat damage; pick up Accuracy later. Krix Domi also skips Multi-Hit Resist on Triniel, takes two levels on the leveling skills only to reach 12, and later picks up every extra Attack and HP node on every board.
  • Priority: blue nodes for Frost, Winter's Shackles, Firestorm.
  • Ariel (new, October 21): a sixth board, for PvE. Its points don't come from levels: Ariel Fragments drop in the Hard mode of Krao Cave and Draupnir (item level 2600) and, since October 28, of Urugugu Canyon and Vakron Sky Island (2700), and 100 fragments become one Daevanion Crystal: Ariel in Substance Morph (Special → Daevanion) — up to 30 crystals from each dungeon, so 120 points per character. Its route is on the Ariel tab of the board below. Azphel stays PvP-only.

Macro & rotation

?The macro key is not bound by default: bind it in Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro. Use the mouse button the guide names — usually RMB, held together with the basic attack on LMB.

AION 2 has two separate tools:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar buttons in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

EUTOPIA plays on stacked lines and presses them himself. In his test on the same character that gave the most damage: about 21,100 DPS, against 19,200 with his macro and 17,400 with one macro for the whole rotation.

2. The rotation

  1. Wish of Concentration — first while it is below 16 (no cooldown cut to waste yet); from 16 it moves to the end.
  2. Element Enhancement — it has the shorter cooldown.
  3. Delayed Explosion, then Winter's Shackles: +20% PvE Damage Boost for 5 seconds.
  4. Hellfire charged to 3/3 inside those 5 seconds.
  5. Fire Wall + Cold Storm.
  6. The core line: Bittercold Wind, Blaze, Firestorm — and Wish of Concentration here, last, once it is 16.
  7. Flame Arrow whenever everything is on cooldown.

Fire Wall and Cold Storm first look great on a training dummy, but a real boss moves around at the start of a fight: EUTOPIA keeps Delayed Explosion and Winter's Shackles first and drops the fields once the boss stands still. On a boss you know is stationary you can swap them.

3. In-game macro — the easy option: three mouse buttons

EUTOPIA's convenient setup plays the whole rotation while you hold three mouse buttons: the macro on RMB, Flame Arrow on LMB (it weaves in between the macro's casts), and Hellfire on a side mouse button — bind it in Key Settings → Quick Slot. Bind the macro in Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro; every step 10 ms:

  1. 1Wish of Concentration10 ms
  2. 2Element Enhancement10 ms
  3. 3Delayed Explosion10 ms
  4. 4Winter's Shackles10 ms
  5. 5Fire Wall10 ms
  6. 6Cold Storm10 ms
  7. 7Bittercold Wind10 ms
  8. 8Blaze10 ms
  9. 9Firestorm10 ms
  • Wish of Concentration at 16: delete it from the top and add it at the bottom, after Firestorm — that is the whole change.
  • Hellfire stays out of the macro. It is a charge skill: from the macro it fires uncharged (in EUTOPIA's earlier test the damage drops by 20–30%). Hold its side button once Winter's Shackles lands — the timing won't be perfect, but the macro carries on around it. Krix Domi casts it by hand too while it is below 16, so the buffs and debuffs land first; once it has the cooldown pick and enough cooldown reduction, he puts it in the macro as well — except in a dungeon full of mechanics.
  • A stacked skill counts. The macro window lists only the skills on the bottom row of the bar, but when a step fires a stacked line, the macro checks every skill on it — so you can keep skills stacked, e.g. Blaze above Winter's Shackles.
  • Flame Scattershot stays out: it only works on a Staggered boss — keep it on its own key.
  • Delay: 10 ms with a ping under 50; 15–20 ms above 50, about 20 ms around 100. Don't add the same step three times in a row — that only adds 30 ms; type the delay you need instead. Afterimages ("shadows") behind your character while the macro runs mean the animations are being cancelled.

The stacked lines above are still the stronger option: in EUTOPIA's October 7 test they did about 10% more damage than one macro. The three-button macro is his October 11 setup for comfort — the hotbar and the earlier tests are read from his October 7 video, the macro window from October 11 (14:28).

4. What you actually press

  • OpenWish of Concentration (while it is below 16) → Element Enhancement → the setup line → Hellfire charged fully.
  • HoldThe core line, and the field line whenever it is ready.
  • LastWish of Concentration at 16, once the big skills are on cooldown.
  • Low manaA few Flame Arrows — keep MP above 50% so both mana passives stay on.

Recap

  • First days: skills at 8–10 with the mana and speed picks; a locked target.
  • Rotation: Wish of Concentration → Element Enhancement → Delayed Explosion → Winter's Shackles → full Hellfire by hand → the fields → Bittercold Wind, Blaze, Firestorm; at Wish 16 it moves to the end. Easy option: the macro on RMB, Flame Arrow on LMB, Hellfire on a side button.
  • Later: Wish of Concentration 16 first, then Flame Arrow, Firestorm, Blaze, Bittercold Wind, Winter's Shackles, Hellfire last; Ice Chain 12 or off the bar.
  • Stigmas: Element Enhancement, Delayed Explosion, Fire Wall, Cold Storm — all to 5, then all to 10, then Element Enhancement to 20.
  • Mana: keep it above 50% — that is damage, not comfort.

How to fight while leveling

  • Packs: Bittercold Wind (root) → Hellfire → Ice Chain until they drop.
  • Elites: any fire hit leaves a Fire Mark → Blaze → Firestorm, kiting with Ice Chain slows.
  • Mana: lock the target so the basic attack keeps running, and weave Flame Arrow.
  • Low HP: Steel Barrier and distance — the Sorcerer has no heal.
