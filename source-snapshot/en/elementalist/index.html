Overview

The Spiritmaster (Elementalist on the Korean and Taiwanese servers) is the only summoner: a spirit fights next to you, every spirit skill gives you an element, and at four elements Elemental Fusion is ready. Most of the damage comes from Elemental Fusion and the spirits — above all Summon: Ancient Spirit and Summon: Fire Spirit.

The build in one sentence

Buff first, then summon: a spirit copies your stats at the moment it appears. Keep five stacks of Cold Shock's Attack buff, open with the buffs and the Ancient Spirit, and let the macro fire Elemental Fusion whenever it is ready.

  • The snapshot rule. A spirit's damage depends on your stats and buffs when you summon it. A buff cast after the summon does nothing for that spirit — never call the Ancient Spirit without your buffs up.
  • Weave the basic attack. Cold Shock gives +2% Attack per hit, up to five stacks. Losing them costs 10% Attack for you and for every spirit you summon; an extra basic attack is worth more than the next summon.
  • Long fights favour you. The class leans on buffs and damage over time, so it gets stronger the longer a fight lasts — and loses the most if you die with everything on cooldown.

Overview

In PvP the Spiritmaster is a kiting class that wins by outlasting: less mobility than a Ranger and less burst than a Sorcerer, but by far the tankiest ranged class, with fear, a dispel and spirits that keep hitting when you are out of range.

  • Fear is control, not a kill tool. Use it to peel and to force out the enemy's Defiance; the damage comes from Elemental Fusion, curses and spirits.
  • Timing decides fights. Know the other class's skills: a defensive used too early or too late loses the fight.
  • Summon: Earth Spirit taunts — and Defiance cannot break a taunt.

The PvP mode follows the "PvP Starter" build of the same guide (updated September 29).

Passives

Passive levels come from every armor piece, the earrings and the necklace, so they run far ahead of the actives — in a finished Season 1 set a passive reaches 30 and more.

First days (aLuckyRO): Spirit Strike first, then Spirit's Descent and Consecutive Countercurrent. Corrode and Element Unification only pay off once your gear gives Critical Hit — before that you barely crit.

Rotation & macro

?The macro key is not bound by default: bind it in Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro. Use the mouse button the guide names — usually RMB, held together with the basic attack on LMB.

Two-hold play: hold Cold Shock on LMB and the macro on RMB, steer, and press the rest by hand. The hotbar line that used to sit on RMB moves to T.

Spam it: Elemental Fusion fires whenever it is ready, then the spirits; Fire Spirit comes last so it stays out the longest.

  2. 2 Flame Blessing
  3. 1 Enhance: Spirit's Benediction

  4. 1 Jointstrike: Corrode

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

2. In-game macro

Bind the macro to RMB, 10 ms on each step:

  1. 1Enhance: Spirit's BenedictionRMB · 10 ms
  2. 2Elemental Fusion10 ms
  3. 3Jointstrike: Corrode10 ms

3. The fight

  1. Open with Flame Blessing → Enhance: Spirit's Benediction → Summon: Ancient Spirit — buffs land before the summon.
  2. Spirit line and Corrode, then start the macro and settle into the two-hold.
  3. Keep rotating your spirits; recast the buffs as soon as they are ready instead of saving them for the Ancient Spirit.
  4. If the five Cold Shock stacks are about to drop, an extra basic attack beats the next summon.

The weave depends on ping: the author wants 65% Combat Speed or more and under ~60 ms ping. aLuckyRO's advice for the first week: play without the macro until you know the class. Bind "dismiss all spirits" to a key you can reach.

Opening a fight

PvP barely relies on the macro: it only weaves Elemental Fusion → Summon: Earth Spirit on RMB. Everything else is timing.

Engaged on

Break the first CC with Defiance at once; be patient with Command: Proxy for the follow-up.

You engage

Enhance: Spirit's Benediction + Kaisinel's Power→Summon: Earth Spirit + Jointstrike: Curse→Summon: Ancient Spirit→Jointstrike: Corrode after their potion

Use Soul's Cry when the Earth Spirit doesn't land a CC, or once their Defiance is gone — root them with Dimensional Control to force it out.

AgainstAnswer
Gladiator, ClericSeize Magic on their invulnerability; Curse first, then Corrode against the sustain.
RangerSeize Magic right after they buff; Corrode against their sustain.
AssassinCommand: Proxy when they engage.
SorcererCommand: Proxy when frozen; save the dash for the meteor — it ignores Evasion and Block.

Overview

The Spiritmaster is the only summoner: a spirit fights next to you, every spirit skill gives you an element, and at four elements Elemental Fusion is ready. Dimensional Control fires each time a spirit uses its skill, and curses keep ticking on the target.

Leveling in one sentence

Level with Summon: Water Spirit (damage and mana), hold Cold Shock for MP, and let the in-game macro cycle the Spirit line and the Fusion line.

Patch Season 1. The build is checked against guides from the global servers: 4 stigmas, cap 20.

How to fight while leveling

  • Spirit: Summon: Water Spirit until the cap — best single-target damage, and it feeds your mana. The others stay at level 1 while leveling.
  • Packs: Jointstrike: Curse (hits up to 4) → Combustion → Elemental Fusion with the AoE pick once it's ready.
  • Mana: the main early problem — a locked target, Cold Shock between skills, and end your spirit rotation on the Water Spirit.
  • Staggered targets: Rapid Scattershot.
  • Skip Soul's Cry — it's a PvP skill.
