AION 2 DPS meters

A DPS meter shows your party's damage, healing and boss HP while you fight. None is built by NC: these are players' programs that read the game's network traffic. Here is what each one offers and where to get it.

Found a mistake or have something to add to this page?

Our pick

LOXIN FreeBeta

Windows 10 / 11 · free, no account needed to use it

The meter the site's author uses: clear overlay, rDPS that credits buffs and heals to their caster, and timers built in.

  • Overlay with five bar styles and a click-through lock
  • Party DPS with rDPS: buffs and heals count for the player who cast them
  • Boss HP and time to kill, death recaps, and your DPS against your own best
  • Fight history, share links, player lookup and kill rankings
  • Event timers (Rift, Shugo Festival, daily reset) and an overlay for OBS
Go to the site loxin.gg

Other meters

Aion2t DPS Meter Free

Windows 10 / 11 · free · 10 languages, Russian and Japanese among them

  • DPS for the whole party, refreshed every half second
  • Per-skill breakdown: hits, crit rate, multi-hits
  • Buff timeline and fight history with filters by boss, date and class
  • Fight replays on the boss's map
  • Downloads from GitHub, Yandex Disk or MEGA
Go to the site aion2t.com

DaevaMeter Free

Windows 10 / 11 · free, no account

  • Damage, DPS, healing and damage taken for the party
  • Boss HP, time to kill and the frenzy countdown
  • Skill breakdown with crits and back attacks, rotation timeline, boss replay
  • Combat Assist: warns about boss casts
  • Records: fastest clears, class DPS and builds from Global boss kills
Go to the site daevameter.online

A2Tools DPS Meter FreeOpen source

Windows (and a Linux guide) · free and open source (GPL-3.0)

  • Fight history with DPS graphs and a skill timeline
  • Ping per fight; works with ExitLag, GearUP and the like
  • Overlay for OBS (browser source)
  • Boss fight uploads, public logs and class stats
  • Plugins: Pet Soul Tracker and event timers
Go to the site a2tools.app

AION2 DPS Meter FreeOpen source

Windows 10 / 11 · free and open source (GPL-3.0)

  • Party DPS, damage share and boss HP, five times a second
  • Per-skill breakdown and hit quality: crit, back, perfect, smite
  • Fight history and field boss respawn timers for your server
  • Share results to chat or Discord
  • English and Russian; the installed copy updates itself
Go to the site GitHub · cyberbadger6969

Aion2DPSMeter FreeOpen source

Windows 10 / 11 · free and open source (GPL-3.0) · any region

  • Live DPS and HPS for the party, fights split on their own
  • History per monster with your best kill time and DPS
  • Per-skill breakdown with damage over time and summons
  • Fight summary for Discord or as a JSON file
  • Opcodes can be fixed by hand after a game patch
Go to the site GitHub · domel1998

AionFlex Free + Pro

Windows · free version, Pro from €4.49 a month

  • Free: live party DPS, boss HP and every skill cast
  • Hall of Champions, boss rankings by class, PvP leaderboard (beta)
  • Character search for Global, Taiwan and Korea
  • Pro: rotation ribbon, saved fights by dungeon or day, fight report charts
Go to the site aionflex.gg

Before you install

  • Every meter here needs Npcap, a free network capture driver (Wireshark uses it too). The meters read the game's traffic; they do not change the game or send anything to it.
  • NC has not approved any third-party meter, and their sites say the same: using one is at your own risk.
  • Download only from the meter's own site or GitHub page - not from re-uploads - and update it after game patches.
  • Don't blame or kick party members for low DPS: NC's policy can punish that.
