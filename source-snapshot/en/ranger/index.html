Overview

The Ranger is a bow DPS that fights from about 20 metres — the range of almost every skill it has. Its damage is top-tier, but survivability and utility are low, so positioning is everything.

The build in one sentence

Buff up, keep Marking Shot (Precision: +300 Critical Hit and +35% Deadshot damage) on the target, and hold three buttons: Snipe, the macro and a charged Deadshot.

This guide follows Whelps — a Ranger main at 1M CP on the Korean servers who also played the global test — and his Season 1 endgame PvE build on questlog.

Patch Season 1. The build is checked against guides from the global servers: 4 stigmas, cap 20.

Passives

Focused Eye (damage boost) and Hunter's Resolve (crit damage) are the only ones that really matter; Hunter's Soul is third.

  • Then the offensive ones: Concentrated Fire, Rooting Eye, Melee Fire.
  • Then Vigilant Eye (Max HP) and Revitalization Contract (heal at 10% HP).
  • Unyielding Resolve and Wind Vigor are nearly useless — level them with leftover points.

Gear & stats

Gear is simple: go for the highest item level. Endgame means crafted weapon, guard and accessories, Ludra bracelets and dungeon armor.

  • Accuracy for the raid: in Korea the target for Ludra was about 1,300 — use it as a guide when you get there.

  • Accuracy is the second stat. Parried hits lose over half their damage, and a Ranger can't always attack from behind. Aim for ~1,100–1,200 (supports add +100).
  • Remaining slots: Attack, Critical Hit or Focused Eye — keep whichever you rolled first. Critical Hit is easier to cap for a Ranger (+300 from Marking Shot).
  • Manastones: one yellow Attack line is enough for Season 1; don't chase front/back attack sets.
  • Only roll soul binds on armor that already has one of the must-haves.
  • Bow and guard at +10 at all times, and basic manastones in every piece (N → Manastones) — cheap raw stats (Fubgun).
  • Where to roll first (Fubgun): weapon and guard, then the rings and the necklace (skill levels, Attack, Might); worth a look later — Skill Speed on gloves, Critical Damage on shoulders. Critical Hit is capped at 50% on global, so you need less of it than Korean guides say.

Daevanion boards

You'll eventually fill almost every board, so don't over-plan. Each blue node gives +1 level to its skill.

  • 1. Blue nodes that take actives to 12 (unlocks their second specialization): Deadshot, Snipe, Gale Arrow, Drill Dart, Burst Arrow.
  • 2. Offensive orange nodes in the board corners — Combat Speed, cooldown reduction. Take the shortest path and grab skills on the way.
  • 3. Attack, Critical Hit, Focused Eye and Hunter's Resolve nodes; then the rest (defense: Defense, HP, Crit Resist).
  • At the very end, route around MP+50 nodes. Resetting a board is cheap.
  • Priority: blue nodes for Deadshot, Snare Shot, Suppressing Arrow, Marking Shot, Defiance.
  • No Ariel on global: there are five boards, and the level-45 one, Azphel, gives only PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — in PvE finish the first four.
  • Azphel (level 45) is all PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — take it, and Triniel earlier.

Stigmas

Level them evenly first — 5 / 5 / 5, then 10 — to hit each breakpoint. Once you have the points for Vaizel's Authority 20, reset and put it there first: that's when the Ranger clicks. Then Bow of Blessing, Supporting Fire and Griffon Arrow to 20 in turn.

  • Four stigma slots, the last at level 37 — no fifth slot at 45 in Season 1, so Explosive Arrow is an alternative for the fourth, not an extra.

Fubgun (global, Oct 15) runs 20 / 15 / 15 / 11 — Vaizel's Authority, Bow of Blessing, Griffon Arrow, Supporting Fire — and puts Supporting Fire last, after Griffon Arrow. His rules: keep Explosive Arrow until Griffon Arrow reaches 15; move Vaizel's Authority to 20 only at about 20 / 10 / 1 / 1 (Vaizel's, Bow of Blessing, Supporting Fire, Explosive Arrow), since Bow of Blessing 5 and 10 add a lot. Vaizel's Authority 20 only pays off with enough Critical Hit — test it on a training dummy before you commit.

Rotation & macro

?The macro key is not bound by default: bind it in Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro. Bind it to RMB — then you hold it together with the basic attack on LMB.

AION 2 has two separate tools, and the build uses both:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar lines in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

Only these two lines matter; they can sit in any hotbar slots.

3. What you actually press

  • OpenerBuffs by hand — Bow of Blessing, Supporting Fire + Vaizel's Authority (one button, same cooldown) — then Marking Shot and a fully charged Deadshot.
  • HoldSnipe on LMB, the macro on RMB and Deadshot on the side button — all three at once.
  • Keep upMarking Shot — recast when Precision drops below ~3s (watch the buff bar). Precision: +5% Perfect Chance, +300 Critical Hit, +35% Deadshot damage. It lasts 15s — recasting it more often is a DPS loss.
  • On cooldownThe buffs again.

Fubgun on a boss: pop the buffs before the pull (their animation does no damage anyway), then Marking Shot → Gale Arrow → Deadshot.

Recap

  • Skills: Deadshot and Gale Arrow 20; Drill Dart, Snipe, Tempest Shot 16; the rest 12; Explosion Trap and Suppressing Arrow only when needed (pulling mobs together, a stun) — in endgame PvE you can skip them; Arrow Scattershot not at all.
  • Stigmas: Vaizel's Authority 20 first, then Bow of Blessing, Supporting Fire, Griffon Arrow; Explosive Arrow as the alternative fourth.
  • Passives: Focused Eye, Hunter's Resolve, then Hunter's Soul.
  • Stats: Accuracy to the cap, then Attack / Critical Hit.
  • Arcana: +4 on Deadshot and Gale Arrow.

How to fight while leveling

Leveling and the first days at 45 follow Moxsy's Ranger guide (global, October 5), made with the help of JohnPal, a Ranger with Korea and Taiwan experience.

  • Packs: most Ranger skills hit only within 4 m of the target, so don't kill mobs one by one. Tag several with Snipe to pull them, then Marking Shot and Explosion Trap to drag them together, then the macro.
  • Elites: Marking Shot first — Precision stays for the whole fight — then the macro, and a fully charged Deadshot on the biggest target. Never waste Deadshot on trash while an elite is up.
  • Stagger: Suppressing Arrow when the boss is close to being stunned. Skip Arrow Scattershot: holding it on a staggered boss does less than the normal rotation.
  • Mana: Snipe restores MP — weave it all the time.

Leveling macro

?The macro key is not bound by default: bind it in Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro. Bind it to RMB — then you hold it together with the basic attack on LMB.

Moxsy's setup (frame 13:32): buffs and bursts live in two lines the macro presses, Snipe and Deadshot stay on the mouse.

8Burst line
  1. 4 Supporting Fire
  2. 3 Bow of Blessing
  3. 2 Vaizel's Authority
  4. 1 Burst Arrow

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Burst Arrow first; when it is on cooldown, every buff fires the moment it is ready.

QGale line
  1. 3 Snare Shot
  2. 2 Explosive Arrow
  3. 1 Gale Arrow

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Gale Arrow, Explosive Arrow, and Snare Shot for the slow that Burst Arrow needs.

4Drill line
  1. 2 Tempest Shot
  2. 1 Drill Dart

In-game order, top to bottom. 1 = highest priority — the bottom slot.

Drill Dart on cooldown, Tempest Shot fills the gaps.

In-game macro, every step with a 10 ms delay:

  1. 1Gale lineQ
  2. 2Drill line4
  3. 3Burst line8
  4. 4Drill line4

The second Drill Dart step does nothing extra yet — in Moxsy's test the damage was the same — but it pays off once the stigmas reach 15. Hold Snipe on LMB together with the macro; Deadshot is charged by hand.
