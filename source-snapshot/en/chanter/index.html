Overview

The Chanter fights in melee with a staff and makes the whole party stronger at the same time: a buffer and a backup healer that every group wants. Don't expect top damage — with equal gear Chanter and Cleric are the two lowest damage dealers — but the party hits harder, dies less and gets its debuffs cleansed.

The build in one sentence

Keep Undefeated Mantra on and hold the macro on the line where Spinning Strike, Marchutan's Wrath and Impactful Crush open Dark Crush again and again, weaving Onslaught between every hit.

How Dark Crush works: it can only be used for a short time after a ranged skill — 2 seconds after Spinning Strike or Impactful Crush, 3 seconds after Marchutan's Wrath. At skill level 16 its own cooldown disappears, so inside that window you press it again and again. Everything in the build is about keeping that window open.

The PvE mode follows Arthars Gaming's Chanter starter guide for the global launch (video, September 26; eight months on the class on the Taiwan server), read frame by frame. Checked against Grobs' Chanter PvE guide (English, global, October 5).

Overview

In PvP the Chanter is a control fighter: a stun on almost every skill, a knockdown that only works on a stunned target, and Dark Crush to finish. Nothing in AION 2 shortens crowd control that lands again and again — five stuns in a row each last their full time — so the whole build is about landing them.

  • Take status-effect chance and duration wherever a skill offers it — Impactful Crush, Wave Blow, Tremor Crush, and Marchutan's Wrath once it reaches 10.
  • Everyone has Defiance, the CC break. Your first job in a duel is to make the enemy spend it; after that, about a minute of their CC immunity is gone and the fight is yours.
  • Your own Defiance: get it to 8 for 10% HP back on use — you always take damage while stunned — and at 12 add the Crushing Strike chain, which can stun too, though the tooltip doesn't say so.

The PvP mode follows notXeon's Chanter PvP guide (video, October 6, global; arena duels against his server's top-ranked Gladiator), read frame by frame. The other skills and their picks stay as in PvE.

Only three skill levels matter: 12, 16 and 20 — that is where a new specialization or slot opens; a level in between adds almost nothing. Skill points give 10, Daevanion +4, the two rings +2, arcana the rest. Get the key skills to 16 first.

Gear & stats

  • Rings: the only place for active skills — four or five lines (crafted rings have one more) that take the key skills from 14 to 16.
  • Weapon and guard: never put skills there — their damage lines are worth more.
  • Passives come from the other pieces. Global gear also rolls Might, Precision and Combat Speed there, so expect fewer passive lines than on Taiwan.
  • Accuracy is short early on: without it you have to hit bosses from behind and get parried.
  • Manastones: Attack, Critical Hit, Accuracy. Guard (off-hand) to +10.

Titles — which one to equip in each slot: After level 45 → Titles. Pets and Genus Insight — what to level and what to roll at 10: Pets → Genus Insight.

Daevanion boards

Daevanion takes your skills from 10 to 14 and adds stats. Each blue node gives +1 level to its skill.

  • Priority: blue nodes for Dark Crush, Onslaught, Spinning Strike and Incandescent Blow, then orange nodes.
  • Priority: blue nodes for Impactful Crush, Wave Blow, Defiance.
  • No Ariel on global: there are five boards, and the level-45 one, Azphel, gives only PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — in PvE finish the first four.
  • Azphel (level 45) is all PvP Damage Boost and Tolerance — take it, and Triniel earlier.

Macro & rotation

?The macro key is not bound by default: bind it in Settings → Key Settings → General → Macro. Bind it to RMB — then you hold it together with the basic attack on LMB.

AION 2 has two separate tools:

  • Hotbar line — several skills stacked on one hotbar button. Pressing it casts the highest-priority skill that's ready, and in AION 2 the bottom slot has the highest priority. The cards show each line top to bottom as in the game.
  • In-game macro — while you hold its key, it presses your hotbar buttons in order and cancels animation tails. Skills on cooldown are skipped.

1. Hotbar lines

Grobs' global setup (frame 19:05): all three openers sit on one line with Dark Crush at the bottom, so after every opener the line goes straight back to Dark Crush; Incandescent Blow has a line of its own.

Dark Crush fires whenever its window is open. When it closes, Spinning Strike opens it again, then Impactful Crush does; Incandescent Blow fills in when both are on cooldown.

Keep Marchutan's Wrath off this line. On its own key it is your reserve: a way to open Dark Crush when both ranged skills are on cooldown, or right after you had to step away from the boss. On the line it would push Incandescent Blow out of the rotation.

  1. 1Dark Crush line10 ms
  2. 2Onslaught10 ms

With this line spam Onslaught and the line key yourself as well — your own presses land between the macro's steps; Focused Defense on big hits.

Where the damage comes from in Arthars' test: Dark Crush and its Piercing Strike follow-up about 40%, then Bursting Blow, Storm Chain, Incandescent Blow and Spinning Strike at about 10% each. That is why Dark Crush goes to 16 first and Onslaught needs Storm Chain.

Fight plan

Bait Defiance, wait, then chain your CC

Impactful Crush→they break it with Defiance→kite→Impactful Crush / Marchutan's Wrath→Wave Blow→Dark Crush + Spinning Strike

Open with Impactful Crush to force out the enemy's Defiance and watch their buff bar: once its icon shows, it is on cooldown for about a minute. Kite until your CC is back — Sprint Mantra makes this easy on the ground — then stun, knock down and burst while they cannot break free.

How to fight while leveling

  • Packs: Rushing Smash in (resets on a kill) → Obliterate → Crush line + Onslaught.
  • Elites: Impactful Crush (stun) → Dark Crush → Wave Blow → Spinning Strike → Dark Crush again.
  • Mana: Onslaught restores MP on every hit — never stop weaving it.
  • Low HP: Recuperation, and Sprint Mantra heals on hits.
