Max two branches: Odyle and the resource of the item you craft first.

Lady Luck only helps when the failure gauge is already high, and it doesn't raise the grade. trueeevil saw it proc about once in 50 nodes at level 10. The first point of a branch also shows its nodes on the minimap.

Which second branch

The resource of the item you want to craft first — usually your weapon.

Odyle

Every craft, wings, Odyle Energy — and morphs into any other resource

If Odyle is cheaper on the market than the resource, buy Odyle and morph it — TitanTheF paid half for his logs this way.

Conquest dungeons leave Odyle nodes after the last boss — another reason to max the branch first.

Each craft can fail and the item breaks — more often the higher the grade. A proc gives the item one grade higher, which you need for the next step.

5

5 stats, not 4

A crafted item rolls five stats, a dungeon item four; with a Philosopher's Stone — six against five.

↑

Inheritance 100%

The next tier is made from your item with a guaranteed success; enhancement and stats carry over.

∞

Never replaced

Craft once, then upgrade the same item patch after patch.

Before you craft

30

Profession 30+

Level the profession on cheap recipes before blue and gold crafts.

%

Real odds

The "+90%" in the craft window is about +9% on top of the base chance (TitanTheF).

♻

Failed blanks

Turn them in to supply orders for Abyss points, sell them or break them down.

Level up fast

  • Level gathering on Sapphire Gemstone: 40–60 veins take you to Novice 50, then the city NPC's green quest (Ruby Gemstone) opens Professional.
  • Craft the cheapest grey items until about Novice 20, then start the chain.
  • Farm pets while you gather — they drop Refining Stones and crafting materials.
  • Answer the anti-bot check when it appears during long gathering sessions.

Making kinah

Source: Aragon

  • Gems sell well while everyone levels Handicrafting: Aragon saw about 2,000 kinah each in the morning, and evening prices run higher.
  • For selling gems he maxes Lady Luck instead of Delicate Touch — a money build, not a crafting one.
  • Don't overpay for ready-made blanks: they still need the expensive materials.
