In short

  • No accounts and no ads. We don't sell or hand over any data.
  • Google Analytics runs only if you allow it in the cookie banner. Until then it is not loaded at all.
  • Our own counters (page views, votes, search) use no cookies and don't store your IP address.

Cookies and browser storage

NameWhat forKept
lang (cookie)The language you picked, so a link without a language opens your version of the page.1 year
Browser storage (localStorage)Your settings on this site: theme, region, open tabs, tracker progress, your cookie choice, a copy of the latest Pet Tracker release. It stays in your browser.Until you clear it; the cookie choice is asked again after 180 days

What our server records

  • Page views and votes: views and visitors per page per day, the “was this helpful” votes and anonymous guide statistics (which mode and step you read; in the roster tracker your classes and number of characters). To count a visitor once a day the server keeps a hash of your IP address and browser made with a secret key — the IP address itself is not stored.
  • Site search: the query, the language, how many results it found and whether you opened one — with no visitor id.
  • Feedback: your text, the page and, if you give one, your contact; it may also record your country (two letters).
  • Screenshots: on the Pet Tracker page you can attach a PNG or JPG to your feedback. The server saves it again from the pixels only (the file's metadata, such as the location in a phone photo, is dropped); only the site owner can open it, and it is deleted with the message, at the latest 60 days after the message was dealt with.
  • Web server logs: IP address, browser and the requested page, for security and error checks. They are deleted after 10 days.

Other services

  • Google Analytics: only with your consent, see above.
  • GitHub: the Pet Tracker page asks GitHub for the latest release, and the download comes from GitHub.
  • Links to YouTube, Twitch and other sites open those sites; their own privacy rules apply there.

Your choice

  • Change your cookie choice any time with Cookie settings at the bottom of every page. “Necessary only” also removes the Google Analytics cookies from this site.
  • Want your feedback deleted, or have a question? Write through the feedback form at the bottom of any page and leave a contact if you want a reply.
